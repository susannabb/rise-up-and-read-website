About us

It started as a conversation between friends.

While volunteering as physicians in refugee camps along the U.S.–Mexico border, we met countless parents worried about their children's health, safety, and future. They spoke of food insecurity, chronic stress, exposure to violence, and limited access to education. Again and again, they shared the same fear: without school, books, and the routines of childhood, their children were falling behind.

Those conversations inspired the creation of Rise Up & Read — a nonprofit dedicated to bringing books, literacy, and hope to children on the move. By creating welcoming libraries, supporting shared reading, and expanding access to multilingual books, we help children rediscover the joy of learning while building the literacy skills and resilience they need to thrive.
