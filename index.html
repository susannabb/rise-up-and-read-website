About Rise Up & Read

A book is a small, portable kind of safety.

Across the Americas and worldwide, children are forced to leave their homes because of violence, poverty, climate disruption, and instability. The journeys that follow are marked by fear and uncertainty, and the chronic stress of displacement - impacting health and wellness.

We bring books to children and families along the migration trail through library clubs, supported libraries, and virtual libraries. We support early literacy as well as strengthen the bond between caregiver and child through creating reading opportunities and shared reading. This is a fundamental way to help children process emotions in the midst of upheaval.

Our mission

"To ease the impact of toxic stress for children on the migration trail by fostering literacy and emotional connection through shared reading."

Stories create vital moments of calm and offer connection, comfort, and a sense of safety when it is needed most.

How we work

Four ways we reach people

We meet families where they are — in the encampments, on their phones, and alongside the people they already trust.

Library clubs

We host library clubs weekly where children come to read, tell stories, and have the chance to just be kids.

Support libraries

We create library spaces in migrant centers so children have a space to read.

Virtual library

We host a virtual library which is projected into 21 migrant centers from Colombia to Mexico, allowing access to stories for children all along the migrant trail.

Service learning

We work with students and volunteers building connections and understanding.

Why it matters

Displacement takes the routines a child needs to grow.

“

Without stable routines or safe spaces, many children struggle to learn, feel secure, and maintain strong emotional connections with caregivers. A book, read together, offers exactly that — a moment of calm and connection.

— Rise Up & Read

Books build language

Shared reading supports early literacy at exactly the age when it matters most — and builds a bridge to reading in new languages along the way.

Books ease stress

A predictable, gentle ritual helps regulate a child's stress response and strengthens the caregiver–child bond in the middle of upheaval.

A library in every pocket

Stories to read together, free on any phone.

Our online library holds stories read aloud in several languages, for families to enjoy together. Reading with your child helps reduce stress, relax, and learn to read.
